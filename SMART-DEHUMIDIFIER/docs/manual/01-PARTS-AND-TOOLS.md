## Chamber & mechanical

- Insulated box (plywood + 25 mm wool/foam, or a steel cabinet) — see 02 §1
- 2 racks (wire mesh) with ~10 cm clearance below/above
- Vent pipes 50–63 mm PVC: one low (intake), one high (exhaust) with rain caps
- Cable glands / grommets where wires enter; electronics mounted OUTSIDE the hot zone

## Tools

- Software side is 100 % free: VS Code/PlatformIO or Arduino IDE 2 —
  **no paid libraries; not even one library to install** (the firmware is
  dependency-free; JSON is generated by built-in code)
- Soldering iron + solder, wire stripper, crimper or pliers
- Multimeter (compulsory — used in every test step)
- USB **data** cable for the ESP32 (charge-only cables cause 90 % of "won't upload")
- Drill, screwdrivers, hot glue / silicone sealant, zip ties
- Optional: clamp meter (coil current), heat gun (shrink tube)

## Spares worth having

- 1 × ESP32, 1 × AHT10, 5 × fuses, spare dupont wires — the parts that
  fail first in field use.
