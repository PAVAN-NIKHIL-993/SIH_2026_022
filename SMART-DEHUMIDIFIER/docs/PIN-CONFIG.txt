============================================================================
 SMART DEHUMIDIFIER PILLAR - PIN CONFIGURATION (firmware v2.0.20
 - S3 remap in: DHT11 33 -> 41, door 34 -> 21)
============================================================================
 Layout: S3 PIN (left)  ->  [PCF pin if the signal goes through an
 expander]  ->  COMPONENT PIN (right).
 Source of truth: src/config.h + variants/esp32-s3/config-s3.h.
 Board: ESP32-S3 DevKitC - N8, N16 or N16R8 (v2.0.13: the pin map
 avoids GPIO 35/36/37, so octal-PSRAM R8 boards work too).
============================================================================


----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. S3 DIRECT WIRING  (no expander - wire straight)
    * = moves to a PCF in the v2.1 plan (section 2)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 S3 PIN                |  ->  COMPONENT PIN
----------------------+-------------------------------------------------------
 GPIO 1   (out)        |  ->  HX711 scale . PD_SCK (CLK)
 GPIO 2   (in)         |  ->  HX711 scale . DOUT
 GPIO 3   (spare)      |  free (no display bridge in this build)
 GPIO 4   (in ADC)     |  ->  battery divider . midpoint (100k/15k)
 GPIO 5   (out)        |  ->  battery divider . 2N7000 gate
 GPIO 8   (SDA)  *bus  |  ->  I2C0 SDA  (AHT10 + PCF #1/#2 + AT24C256 0x50)
 GPIO 9   (SCL)  *bus  |  ->  I2C0 SCL
 GPIO 10  (in)        |  ->  DHT22 . DATA (chamber source #2, cool
                              return; +10k pull-up to 3V3; VCC -> 3V3)
 GPIO 11  (FREE)       |  spare (I2C1 free - future I2C part)
 GPIO 12  (out PWM)    |  ->  555 shifter pin 4 -> 555 pin 3 -> BTS7960 . RPWM
 GPIO 13  (out)        |  ->  BTS7960 . R_EN + L_EN (jumpered); LPWM -> GND
 GPIO 14  (out)        |  ->  P-MOSFET latch . gate (hold HIGH = stay on)
 GPIO 15  (in)      *  |  ->  BUTTON-1 (other leg GND)      [v2.1: PCF#2 P2]
 GPIO 16  (in)      *  |  ->  BUTTON-2 (other leg GND)      [v2.1: PCF#2 P3]
 GPIO 17  (out PWM)    |  ->  L298N . ENB  (fan PWM - the ONLY L298N
                              wire; remove the jumper cap; 10k ENB->GND
                              = fan off at boot; IN3->5V + IN4->GND are
                              tied ON THE MODULE = fixed direction)
 GPIO 18  (in)      *  |  ->  supply optocoupler . OUT   [v2.1: PCF#2 P5]
 GPIO 21  (in)      *  |  ->  door LIMIT SWITCH (other leg GND) [v2.1: PCF#2 P6]
 GPIO 41  (in)         |  ->  DHT11 . DATA (outdoor sensor, +10k pull-up
                              to 3V3, north-side shade; v2.0.20: moved
                              from 33 - this header doesn't break out
                              22-34)
 GPIO 33 / 34           |  NOT EXPOSED on this devkit header (v2.0.20
                              remap: DHT11 -> 41, door -> 21)
 GPIO 35/36/37         |  PSRAM on octal boards (R8) - never wired
 GPIO 39  (in)      *  |  ->  SOLAR toggle (SPDT common)      [v2.1: PCF#2 P4]
 GPIO 48             |  ->  on-board RGB status pixel (WS2812 DATA,
                        |  SPI-driven, zero libs; v1.0 DevKitC. v1.1 = 38!)
 GPIO 40, 42..47, 3, 11 |  FREE - no display in this build (the website is
                        |  the screen: "/" = full control, "/display" = kiosk;
                        |  41 = DHT11, 48 = pixel)
 GPIO 6   (out)     *  |  ->  supply relay . CH1 IN (solar)   [v2.1: PCF#2 P0]
 GPIO 7   (out)     *  |  ->  supply relay . CH2 IN (bypass)  [v2.1: PCF#2 P1]
 GPIO 38  (out)     *  |  ->  buzzer KY-012 . S               [v2.1: PCF#2 P7]
 GPIO 0   (BOOT btn)  |  on-board button: short = start/stop, 2 s = power on
----------------------+-------------------------------------------------------
 AHT10 #1 . SDA/SCL   |  ->  GPIO 8 / GPIO 9   (shares I2C0 with the PCFs)
 DHT22 . DATA          |  ->  GPIO 10   (chamber #2, +10k to 3V3)


----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. VIA PCF8574 EXPANDERS
    both PCFs hang on the SAME I2C0 bus: S3 GPIO 8 -> SDA, GPIO 9 -> SCL
    (VCC = 3V3, GND = GND, INT not connected - polled)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

 PCF #1 @ 0x20  --  FITTED (keypad)
   ADDRESS PINS: A0->GND  A1->GND  A2->GND  (= 0x20)
   PCF PIN | -> COMPONENT PIN
   --------+-------------------------------------------
   P0      | -> keypad ROW 1
   P1      | -> keypad ROW 2
   P2      | -> keypad ROW 3
   P3      | -> keypad ROW 4
   P4      | -> keypad COLUMN 1
   P5      | -> keypad COLUMN 2
   P6      | -> keypad COLUMN 3
   P7      | -> keypad COLUMN 4

 PCF #2 @ 0x21  --  PLANNED (v2.1 FRONT PANEL: mixed outputs + inputs)
   ADDRESS PINS: A0->3V3  A1->GND  A2->GND  (= 0x21)
   10k pull-ups to 3V3 on ALL pins (PCF only ever sinks; HIGH = solid)
   the chip powers up ALL-HIGH -> wiring below makes that mean OFF/quiet
   PCF PIN | -> COMPONENT PIN                       | S3 PIN FREED
   --------+----------------------------------------+---------------
   P0      | -> supply relay . IN1 (CH1 solar feed) | GPIO 6
   P1      | -> supply relay . IN2 (CH2 bypass)    | GPIO 7
   |       | (ACTIVE-LOW relay module - blue board; boot = both OFF)
   P2      | -> BUTTON-1 (other leg GND)            | GPIO 15
   P3      | -> BUTTON-2 (other leg GND)            | GPIO 16
   P4      | -> SOLAR toggle (common; 3V3/GND)      | GPIO 39
   P5      | -> supply optocoupler . OUT            | GPIO 18
   P6      | -> door LIMIT SWITCH (other leg GND)   | GPIO 21
   P7      | -> buzzer LOW-SIDE (+ to 3V3, - to P7) | GPIO 38

 NO PCF #3 NEEDED - one mixed expander carries the whole front panel.

 RULE: buy PCF8574, NEVER PCF8574A (the A answers at 0x38 = AHT10 clash).
 PCF-ABLE  : keypad, relays, buzzer, buttons, toggle, opto, door switch, LEDs
 PCABLE-NOT: RPWM (PWM), battery ADC (analog) + gate (timing chain),
             HX711 (microsecond timing), latch hold (boot-critical),
             AHT10s (I2C devices)

 3. Classic ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin) - direct only (no free pins for PCFs)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 S3-EQUIVALENT       |  -> COMPONENT PIN
---------------------+-------------------------------------------------------
 GPIO 21 / 22        | -> I2C0 (AHT10 #1 + keypad PCF #1 @ 0x20)
 GPIO 32 / 33        | -> I2C1 (AHT10 #2)
 GPIO 25 / 26        | -> BTS7960 RPWM (via 555) / EN
 GPIO 14             | -> L298N ENB (IN3->5V + IN4->GND on the module)
 GPIO 36 / 19        | -> battery divider ADC / gate
 GPIO 13             | -> buzzer
 GPIO 16             | -> P-MOSFET latch hold
 GPIO 15 / 18        | -> BUTTON-1 / BUTTON-2
 GPIO 27             | -> SOLAR toggle
 GPIO 35             | -> supply optocoupler
 NOT WIRED           | door switch (software workflow), supply relay
                     | (manual switching); HX711 CLK/DOUT -> 12/34
                     | (free - no display in this build)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 5. Polarities and levels
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Buzzer            active-HIGH
 Door limit switch closed = LOW (internal pull-up; NO lock fitted)
 Optocoupler       HIGH = selected feed is live
 Relay modules     ACTIVE-HIGH (flip RELAY_ACTIVE_LOW for blue boards)
 Buttons / toggle  to GND, internal pull-ups (LOW = pressed / solar)
 Fan direction     IN3=H + IN4=L = blows OUT (swap pair if reversed)
 Door workflow     software gates: START LOCKED until calibration;
                   mid-cycle door open = instant FAULT


----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 6. Power wiring (not GPIO)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
 550 W panel -> MPPT -> LiFePO4 4S battery (>=50 A BMS)
                              |-> 50 A fuse -> P-MOSFET latch -> 12 V bus
 12 V bus -> BTS7960 B+/B- -> 500 W coil (0.32 ohm)
 12 V bus -> L298N +12V     -> outlet fan
 12 V bus -> 5 V buck (SET 5.0 V FIRST) -> ESP32 + keypad + HX711 + PCFs
 Supply relay CH1 = solar feed to the chamber bus
 Supply relay CH2 = bypass feed (NEVER both channels on)
 Optocoupler LED side across the SELECTED feed
 555 shifter: RPWM pin -> 555 pin 4, 555 pin 3 -> BTS7960 RPWM
 ALL GROUNDS COMMON AT ONE STAR POINT
