# Serials, labels, packaging

## Serial scheme
`PIL-<YYYY>-<NNN>` — e.g. `PIL-2026-001`. Log in the build register:
serial, build date, FW version flashed, QC sheet serial, buyer (later).

## Unit label (printed, laminated, on the controller bay)
```
SMART DEHUMIDIFIER PILLAR
Model SDP-600  ·  Serial PIL-2026-001
Input: 550 W solar panel  ·  Battery: 12.8 V LiFePO4
Heater 500 W max  ·  Chamber max 95 °C (rated 100 °C)
WiFi: AgarbattiDryer  ·  pass: dryer1234  ·  http://192.168.4.1
⚠ Hot surfaces. Ventilate battery bay. Fuse 50 A only.
```

## Small labels
- Fuse holder: `50 A ONLY`
- Battery terminals: `+ / −` (engraved/laminated)
- Coil bay (inside door): `⚠ 500 W — disconnect battery before service`
- Panel lead gland: `PANEL 550 W MAX`

quick-start card (WiFi + first cycle in 5 steps) · QC sheet copy ·
warranty card (12 months, battery 6 months) · spare 50 A fuse ×2.
