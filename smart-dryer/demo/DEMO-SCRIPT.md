# The 10-minute demo (government / investor / buyer)

**Setup before the audience arrives (15 min):** pillar positioned near a
window/door with the panel in sun (or the battery alone — it runs a full
cycle on battery), phone connected to `AgarbattiDryer`, one tray loaded
with wet-but-not-dripping test sticks, serial console open on a laptop
(optional, for the tech crowd).

| Min | Show | Say / do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | The pillar | "One panel, one box, zero fuel, zero internet. Everything inside except the sun." |
| 1–2 | The two bays | Open the door: trays, coil, sensors. Point at the base: "battery lives here, controller at the top — heat rises, electronics don't." |
| 2–4 | The website | Phone → dashboard. "No app, no signal needed — the dryer IS the network. Live temperature, humidity, battery, power." Show the graph building. |
| 4–5 | Full control | Parameters tab: "Every threshold is mine to change — target, cutoff, fans, time. One click back to factory defaults." |
| 5–6 | MAX heat-up | Press Start. MAX HEAT-UP badge, coil glow (dim the lights), fans ramp as humidity climbs. |
| 6–7 | Safety story | "95 °C hard cut, sensors-dead cut, purge before power-off, empty-battery shutdown. Every fault is written down and downloadable." |
| 7–8 | Data | Past cycles → open a finished run → download CSV. "Every batch is evidence — for quality, for buyers, for subsidies." |
| 8–9 | The forecast card + background picker | "It even estimates the weather from the calendar. And I can restyle it —" (pick a background, audience smiles). |
| 9–10 | Roadmap | "Next: it weighs the batch and stops when it's actually dry — no timers. Solar telemetry tells the operator if today is a drying day." |

## Q&A ammo
- **"What if the sun is out?"** — battery (LiFePO4, 2000+ cycles) runs full
  cycles; the panel refills it; forecast card shows the drying window.
- **"What does it cost to run?"** — sunlight. ~500 W for 2–6 h per batch.
- **"Internet?"** — none needed, anywhere. That's the point.
- **"How do I update it?"** — over the air from the same website.
- **"Proof of quality?"** — per-cycle CSV + graphs, every batch.
